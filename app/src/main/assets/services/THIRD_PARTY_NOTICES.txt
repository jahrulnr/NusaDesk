3. Session supervisor — lw-session-supervisor
   File:        lw-session-supervisor
   License:     same as the NusaDesk project (product-owned; not third-party)
   Purpose:     runs as the session tracer's initial tracee: backgrounds
                `systemctl init`, records its real pid and exit marker under

The Python 3 runtime the bridge executes is NOT vendored here: it is a
curated add-on payload of fourteen digest-pinned Ubuntu Noble arm64 .deb
artifacts from ports.ubuntu.com, listed with their digests in
CuratedRuntimeCatalog.guestServiceBridge() and recorded in the activated
overlay's lw-addon-manifest. Ubuntu package licences are documented by the
source packages on ports.ubuntu.com.

The same `guest-service-bridge` overlay installs the compose payload vendored
in the sibling `../compose/` directory: the product-owned
`lw_compose_runtime.py` runner with its `udocker` and `lw-compose-supervisor`
launchers, byte-for-byte udocker 1.3.17 (Apache-2.0) and PyYAML 6.0.1 (MIT)
source tarballs, and the global `lw-compose-supervisor.service` unit enabled
before `systemctl init`. Provenance, licence texts, and the honest runtime
contract are documented in `../compose/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, which is
itself vendored into the overlay at usr/share/lw-services/compose/.
